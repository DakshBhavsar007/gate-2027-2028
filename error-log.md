# GATE CS/IT — Error Log

> **Purpose:** Every wrong answer from PYQs, quizzes, and mocks goes here, tagged by cause.
> This is the single most valuable file for Cycle 2 — it tells you *exactly* what to fix, not what you *feel* is weak.
>
> **Cause tags:**
> - `concept-gap` — didn't know the concept or applied it wrong
> - `silly-mistake` — knew the concept, made a careless arithmetic/reading error
> - `time-pressure` — knew how to solve it but ran out of time
> - `guessed-and-lost` — guessed on an MCQ and lost marks to negative marking
>
> **Fix action:** one-line note on what to do differently (e.g., "re-read paging chapter", "practice modular arithmetic drills", "skip 2-mark MCQs I can't eliminate 2 options on")
